### 🧭 Who I am

Logistics with Nova Poshta, a dealership of machinery from the USA, Europe and China, real estate, a hotel and restaurant complex. Fifteen years of seeing where a business quietly loses money: forgotten leads, manual routine, decisions made blind.

So I don't sell trendy «AI implementations». I look at your business as if it were my own and build automation that pays for itself. **One entrepreneur to another.**
